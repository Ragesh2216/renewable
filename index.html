Project Delivery Method

Four movements,
in the same order,
every time.

The sequence is fixed. It is what makes clean power output predictable, grid-compliant, and bankable.

  1. Audit

    Irradiance & Site Assessment

    Solar irradiance, wind velocity maps, soil resistivity, and grid bay availability are analyzed before sizing equipment.

    Deliverable · Resource Assessment Report
  2. Engineering

    Microgrid Design & PPA Structuring

    BESS sizing, inverter matching, degradation modeling, and long-term fixed tariff PPAs are documented.

    Deliverable · PVSyst Simulation & PPA Draft
  3. Deployment

    EPC Construction & Interconnect

    Solar arrays, turbines, and BESS containers are installed and synced with the regional utility grid.

    Deliverable · Grid Synchronization Certificate
  4. Operations

    O&M & Telemetry Monitoring

    Continuous SCADA monitoring, robotic cleaning, predictive maintenance, and monthly yield reports.

    Deliverable · Live SCADA Dashboard & Yield Audit
